# CommandCanvas 90-second video shot list

Rehearse with a real second browser and keep the critical transformation deterministic by drawing three clearly separated boxes with two arrows.

## 0:00–0:07: camera and opening

**Picture:** Presenter on camera, no product UI yet.

**Line:** “Meetings do not have an idea problem. They have a handoff problem. CommandCanvas lets people and ChatGPT turn rough thinking into structured output on the same live canvas.”

## 0:07–0:15: one-click room

**Picture:** Cut to <https://commandcanvas.vercel.app/demo>. The loaded board, schedule, decision, WebMCP status, activity rail, and two real Presence participants are already visible.

**Line:** “This is a no-signup Supabase room. Daniel and Sarah are connected in two ordinary browsers.”

**Do:** Move Sarah’s cursor briefly from browser B so it appears in browser A.

## 0:15–0:39: primary wow moment

**Picture:** Browser A with the camera preview visible in a small crop if real-hand tracking has passed rehearsal; otherwise use the pointer path without apology.

**Do:** Create a sketch, draw three boxes and two arrows, finish, and select it.

**Say to ChatGPT:** “Make this rough sketch usable as an architecture diagram.”

**Visible reaction:** ChatGPT invokes `transform_sketch`. The rail shows the interpretation in progress. A structured diagram appears beside the original; both remain visible.

**Line:** “The browser rendered those strokes to an image. The model returned schema-validated nodes and edges, and CommandCanvas created a new semantic object without destroying my source.”

## 0:39–0:53: shared physical state

**Do:** Grab the structured diagram and move it. Minimize the rough sketch. In browser B, add a note or task.

**Visible reaction:** Both browsers converge. The receipt rail identifies Daniel, Sarah, and ChatGPT with revisions and sources.

**Line:** “Pointer, hand landmarks, collaborators, and WebMCP all enter the same validated mutation and receipt pipeline.”

## 0:53–1:15: consequential action with human control

**Say to ChatGPT:** “Prepare the meeting packet.”

**Visible reaction:** Exact object content and the editable recipient are shown.

**Do:** Briefly scroll the snapshot, then click **Approve packet**.

**Say to ChatGPT:** “Email this to everyone.”

**Visible reaction:** ChatGPT stages the request. CommandCanvas displays the immutable recipient, content hash, recipient hash, and **SEND** button.

**Line:** “The agent can request the external action. Only the host can authorize it.”

**Do:** Click **SEND**. If Resend is configured and allowlisted, show its real submitted receipt. Otherwise deliberately show **Preview only: not sent** and say, “This public judge environment is intentionally preview-only; it never fakes delivery.”

## 1:15–1:24: WebMCP proof

**Picture:** Fast split shot: the in-page eight-tool list, then the `document.modelContext.registerTool(...)` implementation and phase guard table.

**Line:** “These are native Site Tools against the same live page and session, with schemas, cancellation, lifecycle handling, role guards, and human approval.”

## 1:24–1:30: close

**Picture:** Return to the polished diagram, original sketch, second cursor, and receipt rail.

**Line:** “CommandCanvas turns the meeting itself into the handoff: shared, structured, visible, and reversible.”

## Recording safety checklist

- Use a real browser B for Presence, cursor, and collaborator mutation.
- Do not describe a demo collaborator as a seeded Presence user.
- Do not claim a real email unless the server returns the Resend submitted result.
- Do not claim physical-hand accuracy unless the exact camera rehearsal passes immediately before recording.
- Keep a pointer fallback ready for every gesture.
- Record the WebMCP status and tool proof in the same take or clearly label a stitched implementation shot.
- Reset the demo before the final take and verify revision 3 with exactly three fixtures.
